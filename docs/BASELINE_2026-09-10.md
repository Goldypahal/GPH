## 4. Current Architecture Overview
1. **Camera Ingestion Layer**:
   - Sentinel Integration Contract (Section 39) with catalogue interface `/api/ingest`.
   - Forced TCP transport for RTSP streams; authoritative PTS timestamp preservation.
   - Paced token-bucket ingestion and discontinuity tracker state recovery.
2. **Event Streaming Backbone**:
   - Canonical topics: `camera.sightings`, `camera.health`, `anpr.events`, `tracking.events`, `watchlist.matches`, `correlation.events`, `alerts`, `cases`, `audit`.
   - Distributed EventBus with Kafka KRaft / Redis fallback and DLQ deadlock avoidance (`threading.RLock`).
3. **AI & Vision Pipeline**:
   - YOLO11 vehicle detector, ANPR plate crop detector, PaddleOCR character reader.
   - ByteTrack multi-camera spatial tracklet association with PTS kinematic normalization.
4. **Spatial & Tracking Engine**:
   - Geodesic distance calculation via WGS-84 ellipsoidal math (`geopy.distance.geodesic`).
   - Configurable physical plausibility velocity thresholds (`IMPOSSIBLE_SPEED_THRESHOLD_KMH=180.0`, `SUSPICIOUS_SPEED_THRESHOLD_KMH=130.0`).
5. **Security & Identity Architecture**:
   - OIDC / Keycloak integration with RS256/ES256 JWKS signature verification.
   - Attribute-Based Access Control (ABAC) with clearance levels (`TOP_SECRET` to `UNCLASSIFIED`) and district fences.
   - Strict production auth: Prohibits anonymous or developer bypass fallbacks when `ENVIRONMENT=production`.
   - Root model validators for `SECRET_KEY` ($\ge 32$ chars) and `MINIO_SECRET_KEY`.
6. **Forensic Evidence & Custody Vault**:
   - WORM storage drivers (Local and MinIO Object Lock) rejecting overwrite (409) and deletion (403).
   - SHA-256 byte-level seal verification and append-only custody logging.
   - Statutory disclaimers conforming to Section 65B Indian Evidence Act / Section 63 Bharatiya Sakshya Adhiniyam.
7. **Telemetry & Operations**:
   - Prometheus exporter at `/api/system/metrics` exposing live probes (`postgres_health`, `redis_health`, `minio_health`, `db_query_latency`, frame counters, DLQ size).
   - 26-subsystem deployment readiness audit at `/api/system/readiness`.

## 5. Known External Dependencies (Physical/External)
The software implementation is fully complete and self-healing. Physical deployment requires:
1. **Gujarat State Data Centre (GSDC) Infrastructure**: Physical K8s cluster, PostgreSQL HA (Patroni), Kafka KRaft, and MinIO Object Lock storage.
2. **GSWAN Network & Government Gateways**: Dedicated VPN / NIC connections and mTLS credentials for live VAHAN, SARATHI, eGujCop, AFIS, and NAFIS production environments.
3. **Multi-Vendor Physical CCTV Grid**: Live RTSP camera feeds across 33 Gujarat district police control rooms.
4. **Formal CA Enrollment**: Forensic Science Laboratory (FSL) / Gujarat Forensic Sciences University digital certificate authority enrollment for Section 65B/63 court signatures.
